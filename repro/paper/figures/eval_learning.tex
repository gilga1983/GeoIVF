\caption{\textbf{Learning, demand locality, and starting points on PubMed.} Mean logical SSD-read savings relative to junction-only routing at six matched Recall@10 targets. Both panels compare $k\in\{1,2,4,8\}$ with $B=14{,}464$ and $C=2{,}048$ fixed. (a) Savings after increasing causal history prefixes, each evaluated on its next 500 requests. (b) Savings across five controlled working sets, each with 4,000 warm-up and 1,000 measured requests. Negative values indicate additional reads.}
\Description{Two line plots show logical SSD-read savings relative to junction-only routing, using consistent markers and line patterns for 1, 2, 4, and 8 historical starts. Left: after zero history, two starts save 3.27 percent, whereas eight starts incur 1.92 percent more reads; four starts lead during much of the warm-up, before eight narrowly catch up at 4,000 previous queries. Right: at high query repetition, two starts still save 4.14 percent, while eight increase reads by 1.79 percent.}
